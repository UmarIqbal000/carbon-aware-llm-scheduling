## 2. Defensible Research Gap Statement

Across the 13 full-text surveys and 3 additional abstract-verified syntheses audited from 2023–2026, research has bifurcated into two disconnected silos:

1. **Cloud & Serverless Carbon-Aware Scheduling** (Hewage et al. 2026; Yang et al. 2025; Alam et al. 2026; Agarwal et al. 2026; Besozzi et al. 2025) models spatiotemporal renewable matching, container autoscaling, generic FaaS cold-start vs. idle energy trade-offs, and—only in Hewage et al.—embodied carbon for monolithic latency-sensitive microservices. **Zero** of these surveys examine autoregressive LLM phase separation (`prefill` vs. `decode`), KV-cache capacity/transfer overhead, or token-level SLOs (`TTFT`, `TPOT`).
2. **Efficient LLM Inference Serving & KV Infrastructure** (Miao et al. 2025; Bai et al. 2026; Jiang et al. 2026; Pan & Li 2025; Sisodia 2026; Li et al. 2024) formalizes prefill–decode disaggregation, continuous batching, paged/tiered KV caching, and ServerlessLLM model loading for throughput, GPU cost, or TTFT/TPOT latency. **Virtually none** incorporate time- and region-varying grid carbon intensity (`gCO2eq/kWh`), SLO-constrained cross-region carbon routing, serverless GPU idle vs. cold-start emission break-even points, or amortized embodied carbon across heterogeneous accelerator pools.

This paper anchors specifically at **five unbridged mechanisms** at their intersection:
1. **Phase-Disaggregated Carbon Scheduling:** Compute-bound `prefill` and memory-bandwidth-bound `decode` phases exhibit distinct power draw profiles (`P_prefill` vs. `P_decode`), batching scaling, and hardware affinities, creating unexplored opportunities to schedule or colocate prefill and decode pools across grids with differing carbon intensities and renewable availability.
2. **KV-Cache Retention vs. Migration vs. Recomputation Energy-Carbon Trade-offs:** Offloading, pinning, or transferring large KV tensors over inter-node or cross-region links consumes interconnect energy and pins high-power GPU HBM (`300–700 W` envelope), creating a coupled latency–energy–carbon trade-off between warm KV persistence, cross-pool KV transfer, and stateless recomputation on greener nodes.
3. **Decoupled Token-SLO Carbon Routing:** Interactive workloads tolerate minimal `TTFT` delay (`~0.2–2 s`) and tight `TPOT`, whereas asynchronous/batch reasoning prompts permit temporal deferral; routing frameworks must enforce hard per-phase `TTFT`/`TPOT` percentiles rather than treating requests as uniform job deadlines.
4. **Serverless GPU Cold-Start Carbon Penalties:** Unlike lightweight CPU FaaS containers, scale-to-zero LLM replicas load multi-gigabyte weights and initialize CUDA/KV arenas over multi-second cold starts, flipping the energy/carbon break-even point between warm keep-alive idle draw and cold reload emissions as grid carbon intensity fluctuates.
5. **Embodied vs. Operational Carbon Amortization:** `12 of 13` inspected full-text surveys have `0` mentions of `embodied` carbon; sizing and routing across newer high-TDP accelerators (higher embodied footprint, high prefill efficiency) vs. older/memory-abundant accelerators (lower amortized embodied cost, decode pooling) remains unquantified under realistic utilization regimes.

## 3. Research Questions

- **RQ1 (Taxonomic Synthesis):** How do 2023–2026 cloud/serverless carbon-aware scheduling strategies and phase-aware LLM serving systems map onto a unified comparative taxonomy spanning phase granularity, KV-cache policy, SLO formulation, serverless lifecycle control, and operational/embodied carbon boundaries?
- **RQ2 (Phase Disaggregation & KV-Cache Carbon Trade-offs):** Under dynamic regional grid carbon intensity and hard TTFT/TPOT SLO constraints, how do colocated continuous batching, disaggregated prefill–decode pooling, and prefix/KV-transfer policies compare on operational carbon (`gCO2eq/token`), energy efficiency (`J/token`), and tail SLO violation rate?
- **RQ3 (Serverless GPU Keep-Alive vs. Cold-Start Penalty):** Under bursty serverless request arrivals and multi-gigabyte model checkpoint loading, across what inter-arrival rates and grid carbon intensities does idle GPU keep-alive carbon exceed cold-start reload carbon, and what dynamic carbon-aware keep-alive window minimizes combined cold-start and idle emissions while preserving TTFT SLOs?
- **RQ4 (Operational vs. Embodied Carbon Amortization):** When amortized hardware embodied carbon is co-accounted alongside operational electricity emissions across heterogeneous GPU tiers, how do utilization levels and regional grid carbon intensity shift the Pareto frontier between colocated, homogeneous disaggregated, and heterogeneous prefill–decode deployments?

## 4. Honest Contribution Statement

- **Unified Cross-Domain PRISMA Synthesis & Comparative Taxonomy:** Conducts a transparent PRISMA-aligned systematic review bridging carbon-aware cloud/serverless scheduling and phase-aware LLM inference serving, classifying existing primary systems across workload phase, KV-cache state management, spatiotemporal/DVFS control levers, SLO guarantees, and lifecycle carbon scope.
- **Cross-Cutting Gap & Compatibility Audit:** Systematically identifies structural assumptions in current FaaS/Kubernetes carbon schedulers (e.g., stateless task migration, small-image cold-start models, deadline-agnostic deferral) that fail or invert under stateful, phase-asymmetric, multi-GB GPU LLM serving.
- **Reproducible Parameterized `SimPy` Simulation Study:** Implements and releases an open, deterministic discrete-event simulation (`SimPy`) driven by documented hardware power/latency parameters and regional grid carbon traces to evaluate colocated vs. phase-disaggregated serving, carbon-aware routing, dynamic serverless GPU keep-alive windows, and operational-plus-embodied carbon sensitivity—explicitly scoped as a reproducible comparative system study rather than a production multi-cluster deployment.
