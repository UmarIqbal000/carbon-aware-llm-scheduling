## 2. Problem Addressed
Hardware architects and systems designers lack validated analytical tools to quantify manufacturing/embodied carbon tradeoffs early in architectural design space exploration.

## 3. Method / Architecture / Control Mechanism
*(Access Level: `abstract_only` — paywalled ACM ISCA 2022 proceedings; strictly bounded to verified Crossref/OpenAlex abstract and metadata.)* Introduces *ACT*, an architectural carbon footprint modeling tool capturing manufacturing and infrastructure embodied emissions alongside operational efficiency to support sustainability-driven design optimization structured around Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle.

## 4. Dataset, Workload, Benchmark & Hardware Platform
*(Abstract-only record.)* Constructs illustrative hardware design-space use cases centered on Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle principles; exact fabrication parameters and chip evaluations are omitted from the abstract.

## 5. Key Quantitative Results
*(Abstract-only record.)* Verified abstract demonstrates that optimizing hardware architecture specifically for total lifecycle carbon yields markedly different design choices compared to optimizing purely for performance or operational energy efficiency.

## 6. Limitations
*(Abstract-only record.)* Exact fab emission equations, die/memory parameters, and validation margins cannot be audited behind ACM paywall (partially complemented by open-access `faiz2024llmcarbon` and `li2025ecoserve`).

## 7. Relevance to Research Questions (RQ1–RQ4)
Key modeling lineage anchoring RQ1 and RQ4 (`Reduce/Reuse/Recycle` embodied carbon framework later extended by `EcoServe` and `LLMCarbon`).
