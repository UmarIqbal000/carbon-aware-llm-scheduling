Interactive large language model (LLM) inference separates into compute-bound prefill and memory-bound decode stages across multi-region cloud clusters and bursty serverless GPU pools. A PRISMA-2020-guided review of 61 works shows that while recent systems address subsets in isolation (including single-cluster disaggregated GreenLLM and colocated multi-datacenter carbon routers), no implemented system jointly integrates prefill/decode disaggregation, wide-area key-value (KV) transfer gating, host and GPU embodied carbon amortization, and serverless checkpoint keep-alive, and 75.4\% of reviewed studies omit manufacturing emissions. To evaluate this design space, I formalize an ISO/IEC 21031:2024 Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) control architecture in a trace-driven SimPy 4.1.2 discrete-event simulator validated against published serving and loader baselines. Over 10 paired seeds driven by Azure LLM traces, UK NESO, and Japan TSO grid telemetry, hybrid routing lowers operational SCI by up to 60.8\% and total SCI by up to 54.9\% on conversational traffic at 0.81\%--0.82\% SLO violations, and reduces bursty-code SLO violations by 85.2\%--86.0\% below unconditional geographic disaggregation. On wind-clean grids, amortized hardware reaches 93.0\%--97.6\% of total SCI, causing four spare clean nodes to raise lifecycle emissions by 27.7\%--32.6\%.
