Interactive large language model (LLM) inference separates into compute-bound prefill and memory-bandwidth-bound decode stages across multi-region cloud clusters and bursty serverless GPU pools. A PRISMA-2020-guided review of 65 works shows that while recent systems address subsets in isolation (including single-cluster disaggregated GreenLLM and colocated multi-datacenter carbon routers), no implemented system jointly integrates prefill/decode disaggregation, wide-area key-value (KV) transfer gating, host and GPU embodied carbon amortization, and serverless checkpoint keep-alive, and 76.9\% of reviewed studies omit manufacturing emissions. To evaluate this design space, I formalize an ISO/IEC 21031:2024 Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) control architecture in a trace-driven SimPy 4.1.2 discrete-event simulator validated against published serving benchmarks and held-out scaling trends. Over 10 paired seeds driven by Azure LLM traces, UK NESO, and Japan TSO grid telemetry, hybrid routing lowers operational SCI by up to 60.8\% and total SCI by up to 54.9\% on conversational traffic at 0.81\%--0.82\% SLO violations, prunes cross-region KV pipelining below 0.31\% even on 400~Gbps links, and reduces bursty-code SLO violations by 85.2\%--86.0\% below unconditional geographic disaggregation. On wind-clean grids, amortized hardware reaches 93.0\%--97.6\% of total SCI, causing four spare clean nodes to raise lifecycle emissions by 27.7\%--32.6\% at nominal load before turning carbon-reducing (-30.6\% to -36.5\% at 3.0x) once demand crosses a 1.6x--1.7x break-even threshold.
